# True Bill — Brand Bible

*Special Wagers Unit · Office of Special Prosecutions*

> **A courtroom for your bets.** Every play is indicted on the evidence,
> timestamped before tip, and tried in public against the closing line.
> *Res ipsa loquitur* — the evidence speaks for itself.

This is the single source of truth for the True Bill brand: voice, visual
system, lexicon, and the marketing playbook. It was developed from a wave of
dedicated research (trust patterns in picks products, the Law & Order verbal
& visual identity, audience needs, naming collision, WCAG contrast, and
responsible-gambling compliance). The theme is design-and-vibe only — it never
touches the model, the data, or the math. The discipline is the product; the
courtroom is the wit. **Never let the bit outrun the numbers.**

---

## 1. The name

**True Bill** (always two words). A grand jury returns a *true bill* when it
finds the evidence supports an indictment — i.e. *the case is real.* That is
exactly what the model does: it only files a charge when the edge clears the
bar. The opposite is a *no bill* (insufficient evidence — a pass).

- Always render **True Bill**, never "Truebill" (the one-word form is the
  retired fintech app, now Rocket Money — keep the space and you read as the
  legal term, not the budgeting app).
- Lead creative with the **gavel, not the wallet**, to outrun any residual
  finance-app echo.
- Category is open in betting/entertainment (Class 41). Domain canon:
  **truebill.bet** (defensive: getatruebill.com, thetruebill.com).

## 2. Positioning

- **One-liner:** True Bill is the Special Wagers Unit — a courtroom for your
  bets, where every play is indicted on the evidence, timestamped before tip,
  and tried in public against the closing line.
- **Category we own:** not "picks," not a tout service — **the betting record
  of public record.** Everyone else sells outcomes ("we hit 73%!"). We sell
  process and accountability: an immutable, timestamped docket.
- **The honest claim (our "Winners Welcome"):** **"We lose. It's on the
  record."** Every DISMISSED verdict and every graded loss stays in the
  Conviction Record forever, next to the wins. The flex isn't the win rate —
  it's that the People beat the closing line whether the bet won or not. A
  tout can't copy this without becoming us.

## 3. Verbal identity

### Voice — five postures
1. **Measured**, not breathless. State the edge and its size; never raise your
   voice to sell it.
2. **Procedural**, not improvised. Every verdict follows the same process, so a
   word always means the same thing.
3. **Dryly witty**, not zany. The joke is in the deadpan and the restraint,
   never in exclamation points.
4. **Candid**, not spun. Publish losses with the same face as wins.
5. **Respectful of the reader**, not paternalistic. Assume an intelligent adult
   who understands risk and decides for themselves.

### Tone shifts
- **Confident play:** quietly assured — let the numbers carry the swagger.
- **A pass (DISMISSED):** unbothered, almost proud. "Discipline is a pick too."
- **A losing day:** flat, accountable, forward-looking. No excuses, no flinching.
- **Responsible-gambling moments:** drop the bit almost entirely. Plain, warm,
  sincere. *The closer the copy gets to a person's money or wellbeing, the more
  the costume comes off.*

### Taglines
- **Primary:** *True Bill — when the evidence clears the bar.*
- Rotating: *We don't pick — we prosecute.* · *Indict the edge. Dismiss the
  rest.* · *No filing without evidence.* · *The People bet probabilities, not
  feelings.* · *The market rests. We cross-examine.* · *Winners welcome. Touts
  dismissed.*
- **Sign-off:** **"The evidence is in. Return your verdict."**
- **Motto (Latin):** ***Res ipsa loquitur*** — "the evidence speaks for itself."

### The cold-open narration (landing)
> In the sports-wagering system, the bettor is protected by two separate yet
> equally important groups: **the model**, which weighs the evidence, and **the
> market**, which sets the price. These are their stories.
>
> Every night, a docket of games comes across the desk. Most are fairly priced
> and walk free. A few are not — and on those, the People file.
>
> This is True Bill. The Special Wagers Unit. The evidence is on the table.
> The verdict is yours.

### Voice do / don't
- **Do** quantify the edge ("~54% to cover, a 2-point edge"); **don't** "🔥 strong tonight."
- **Don't** ever promise outcomes; no "lock," "guaranteed," "sure thing."
- **Do** own losses in the same voice as wins; never quietly delete one.
- **Don't** let the bit bury the information.
- **Do** drop the theme entirely for responsible-gambling/safety copy.
- **Don't** insult the market or the reader.
- **Do** keep terms consistent — INDICTED = play, DISMISSED = pass, always.
- **Don't** theme the *bettor* as a convict — theme the *model* as prosecutor.

## 5. Visual system

### Palette (the "Case File" — escapes the casino green/gold sea)
Sober-premium: parchment + brass scales + precinct blue + oxblood stamp on warm
near-black. All values are CSS variables in `app/theme.py` (so the whole app —
including custom HTML cards — re-skins from one place, and a sidebar toggle
flips back to the original neon).

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#0a0a0b` | app background (warm near-black) |
| `--card` | `#16140f` | manila-in-shadow card |
| `--panel` | `#111014` | raised panel |
| `--line` | `#2b281f` | borders / hairlines |
| `--acc` | `#c69a3f` | **brass** — scales of justice; brand & primary action |
| `--acc2` | `#5e87b3` | **precinct blue** — the show's neon-glow; info/links |
| `--pos` | `#6f8b3a` | olive "convicted" green — win / over |
| `--neg` | `#b23b3b` | oxblood — **fills/stamps only** (fails AA as text) |
| `--neg-text` | `#cf6a6a` | oxblood **text tier** (AA-safe) — loss numbers/labels |
| `--warn` | `#cf9a33` | amber — caution / NOTE |
| `--text` / `--text2` | `#ece1cb` / `#d7ccb4` | parchment text tiers |
| `--muted` / `--muted2` | `#9c9279` / `#8a8068` | label text (floor for AA) |
| `--faint` | `#6e6555` | **non-text chrome only** (3.2:1) |

**Contrast rule:** `--muted2` (#8a8068, 4.7:1) is the dimmest token allowed for
real text; oxblood/info as small text must use the `-text` tiers. Brass and blue
are **brand, not data** — in a chart, win/loss is `--pos`/`--neg`, never
brass-vs-blue, so the accent stays meaningful.

### Type — three families, three jobs
- **Cinzel** (carved, Trajan-derived) — display only: title cards, headers, the
  wordmark. All-caps, tracked, short. Never in sentences.
- **Courier Prime** (typewriter) — the document: all numbers, odds, timestamps,
  metric values, stamp labels. Tabular.
- **Inter** — everything a human reads in sentences: body, help, alerts.

### Logo
- **Primary:** `TRUE·BILL` in Cinzel 600, all-caps, +1.5px tracking, with a
  **brass interpunct** (`·`, the fulcrum of the scales) as the only color. Single
  ink, no gradient on the formal mark.
- **Monogram `TB`** in a brass-ruled square (an evidence tag) for favicon/avatar.
- **Figurative mark (utility only):** a minimal **balance scale** as a single
  brass stroke (beam + pin, no pans). **Never a gavel** (cliché; a gavel is the
  judge's tool, but a "true bill" is the grand jury's act — the scale is the
  honest metaphor).

### Iconography & motion
- **Rubber stamps** are the signature element: `TRUE BILL` (olive), `INDICTED`
  (brass), `DISMISSED` (oxblood, double-rule), `EXHIBIT A` (blue), `SEALED`
  (oxblood). **One stamp per object, max.**
- **The dun-dun** ("The Clang") — a two-pulse snap + an opt-in Web-Audio sting
  (metallic clang + low thud). Never autoplays; mutable; fires only on explicit
  click or once on a fresh verdict reveal.
- **Stamp slam** — the verdict stamp enters oversized + transparent, slams down,
  overshoots, settles at −4°. Fires once.
- **Title cards** — white-on-black intertitles (the *only* place pure black is
  used): eyebrow (jurisdiction) · Cinzel title (the matter) · mono sub
  (date · slate · count). Snap-in from the left.
- All motion honors `prefers-reduced-motion`. **The theme serves the numbers:**
  flat plot areas, zero-baseline magnitude charts, no brass glow inside a chart.

### Anti-cheese budget
One stamp per object · red string in exactly one view · Cinzel never in
sentences · brass/blue reserved for brand · pure black only on title cards ·
the scale loader settles and stops · sound is a garnish, never a notifier. If a
screen has more than ~2 thematic flourishes competing, cut to one.

## 6. Marketing & growth

### The verdict pick-card (the core organic unit)
A 1080×1350 (4:5) card built to be screenshotted without crop. Top to bottom:
masthead + sequential **Docket No.** → the play as a case caption (*The People
v. Atlanta Bullpen*) → CHARGE + **filed-at price** → the **verdict stamp** →
**The Evidence** (2–3 plain bullets) → stake band (units · ¼-Kelly · strength) →
the **Conviction Record strip** (sample size first: ROI · units · CLV ·
beat-close%) → watermark + **filed-at timestamp to the minute**. The trust strip
and timestamp are non-negotiable — they *are* the proof. A **REDACTED** variant
seals the charge (`[SEALED]`) but keeps the Evidence + verdict + record visible:
the paywall becomes the ad. Generate one with `scripts/make_verdict_card.py`.

### The Briefing (daily/weekly)
Same time every day ("The Docket drops at eleven"). Sections: the calendar →
The Indictments → **The Court Passes** (what we declined, and why — the trust
engine) → Standing on yesterday (graded, **with CLV regardless of result**) →
The Record. Weekly **Closing Arguments** recap leads with the honest score and
an **"On appeal"** line — a loss where CLV was still positive. Push copy points
at value the user came for ("your docket is ready"), never manufactures urgency,
capped 1/day.

### Onboarding — The Arraignment (5 steps)
You're being arraigned → how to read a verdict → units & ¼-Kelly (bet your own
bankroll) → **CLV: the only number that can't be faked** (judge us by the close)
→ Summon the court (referral, baked in last — after four screens of *giving*).

### Growth loops
- **Subpoena a friend / Summon a juror** — finite invites ("sealed seats");
  a real-time **Gallery** tracker (sworn-in vs. awaiting service).
- **The Record** leaderboard ranks *participation in the process* (tenure,
  attendance, referrals) — **never P&L.** Tiers: Gallery → Juror → Foreperson →
  Clerk of the Court.
- **Citations & Commendations** — shareable achievement cards (Sworn In,
  Foreperson, attendance streaks), each carrying the Conviction Record strip.

### The Firm (About)
True Bill started as one analyst, one spreadsheet, and one frustration: every
"expert" sold winners and buried losers. So we built the opposite — a docket
where every play is charged, timestamped, and entered into a public record
before tip. We can't quietly delete the bad ones. That constraint is the whole
product. **"The People"** is the model; it compares its probability to the best
price and files only when the gap is real. We live or die on **Closing Line
Value**, size in **units** at **¼-Kelly**, and we will never tell you to bet the
rent. *True Bill is a research and entertainment product — not financial advice,
and not a guarantee.*

## 7. Responsible gambling & compliance

We're a content product, not a sportsbook — but we adopt the operator bar
(AGA / ASA / NCPG) voluntarily, because it's the line between "credible model"
and "tout."

- **Standing disclaimer (inline):** *Model estimates, for entertainment only.
  Not betting advice. 21+. No outcome is guaranteed. Gambling problem? Call or
  text 1-800-GAMBLER.*
- **Banned words → on-brand substitutes:** "guaranteed/lock" → "our strongest
  case (not a guarantee)"; "risk-free" → "every position carries variance";
  "easy money" → "an edge is small and slow"; "bet now" → "the line will move;
  decide on your own clock."
- **Integrate, don't bury:** RG copy on the landing, in onboarding, on a
  high-confidence play ("strong cases still lose"), on a losing streak (proactive
  check-in, never a bounce-back upsell), and in a permanent **Play Within
  Limits** panel (helpline, self-exclusion, NCPG).
- **Variance realism:** sustained ROI of 2–5% is solid, 5–10% is strong; 30%+
  ROI or 65%+ win-rate claims are red flags. Report the full sample, losing
  weeks included.
- **Theme boundary (crime motif):** keep the wit on *rigor and verdict*; never
  joke about doing time, sentences, debt, or ruin, and never frame the *bettor*
  as a convict. The user is a careful juror weighing evidence — never a gambler,
  an addict, or a defendant.

### Play Within Limits
National Problem Gambling Helpline — call or text **1-800-GAMBLER**, text
**800GAM**, or chat at **ncpgambling.org/chat**. Free, confidential, 24/7. Most
states offer voluntary **self-exclusion**. Set your time and money caps before
you start. 21+ where legal.
